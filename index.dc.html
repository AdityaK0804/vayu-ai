HOW THE AI WORKS

From raw signal to clean-air action in three steps

01 — INGEST

Fuse every signal

41 ground sensors, satellite aerosol columns, traffic feeds and meteorology stream in every minute and are quality-checked automatically.

02 — PREDICT

Model the plume

A graph neural network learns how pollution travels between neighbourhoods and forecasts each pollutant 72 hours ahead.

03 — INTERVENE

Recommend & measure

The engine ranks interventions by modelled AQI reduction and cost, then tracks the real outcome after you act.
